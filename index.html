Important: This is an estimate of gross pay before deductions. It assumes a daily-paid employee with an 8-hour basic workday and standard Philippine holiday premiums. A regular holiday not worked is paid only if eligibility conditions are met; special non-working days generally follow no-work-no-pay unless a more favorable policy applies. Night differential is calculated only on the night hours entered (10 PM–6 AM), including eligible overtime, at 10% of the applicable hourly pay. It does not automatically detect cross-midnight holiday changes, overlapping holidays, other special arrangements, or payroll deductions. For a non-worked rest day, the result is zero unless an eligible regular holiday applies. Confirm actual payroll treatment with HR or DOLE.
